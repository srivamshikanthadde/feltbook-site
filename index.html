Keep score, not the cash.

A dead-simple scorekeeper for friendly home poker games — track buy-ins and cash-outs, and see exactly who pays whom at the end.

🃏 Live buy-in tracking 🧮 Auto settlement 👥 Guests & clubs 📈 Your results history

No money changes hands in the app

  • FeltBook is a ledger, not a wallet — it never processes, holds, or moves money.
  • At the end of a game it computes the fewest transfers to settle up; people pay each other directly, however they like.
