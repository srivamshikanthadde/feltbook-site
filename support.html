Common questions

  • Does FeltBook handle money? No. It only keeps score. Payments happen directly between people, outside the app.
  • How do I add someone to a game? The game's manager or owner can add club members, or add a guest by name.
  • How do I delete my account? Open Profile → “Delete account”. Your personal data and results are removed; past shared game records are kept but anonymized.
  • How are settlements calculated? Once cash-outs are entered and the game is locked, the app computes the minimal set of transfers so everyone is squared up.
